Taking the Next Step

 

Choosing to enter a structured sober living environment takes courage. Our admissions process is straightforward, confidential, and focused on ensuring POTHI is the right fit for your recovery goals.

 

Who We Serve

 

Provision Of The Hearts INC is designed for individuals committed to abstinence and personal growth who are ready to embrace a structured, 12-step oriented lifestyle as they transition toward independent living.

 

3-Step Admissions Process

  1. Confidential Conversation: Call or email our admissions team to discuss your current stage of recovery, background, and goals.
  2. Program Alignment: We review house rules, AA/NA expectations, and bed availability together to confirm mutual alignment.
  3. Move-In & Orientation: Once approved, we coordinate your arrival date, welcome you to the house, and help you settle into your daily routine.

 

What to Bring (Packing List)

 

Because POTHI provides food, toiletries, laundry detergent, linens/kitchenware, and utilities, packing is simple.

Recommended Items:
  • Government-issued photo ID (driver’s license, state ID, or passport)
  • Comfortable everyday clothing for 7–10 days (plus interview/work attire if seeking employment)
  • Approved, non-narcotic prescription medications in their original pharmacy bottles
  • Personal recovery literature (AA Big Book, NA Basic Text, journal, pens)
  • Cell phone and charger (subject to house guidelines)

 

Prohibited Items (Do Not Bring):
  • Any alcohol-based products (including mouthwash, cologne, perfume, or hand sanitizer containing alcohol)
  • Aerosol sprays or harsh chemicals
  • Unapproved over-the-counter medications, supplements, or narcotic prescriptions
  • Weapons, candles, incense, or drug/alcohol paraphernalia
